### Should you take it?

Compute cost: irrelevant. The real questions are:

1. **Can you finish in 2 hours?** — 50K input is a medium codebase. Identifying a flaky test requires reading test output patterns, timing issues, non-deterministic state. That's reasoning work, not just token generation.

2. **Do you actually know auth-service?** — Flaky tests are notoriously context-dependent. Race conditions, shared DB state, unmocked external calls. Without domain familiarity you could burn the full 2 hours.

3. **Platform overhead** — Does the bounty platform take a cut? Does Hermes take a fee? That affects the real number.

**Bottom line:** At $0.006–0.009 per run, you could attempt this bounty 1,500+ times and still break even. Take it if you can finish the work — the compute economics are a rounding error.
