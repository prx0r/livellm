the problem
LLM model prices change faster than static knowledge can keep up. An agent making routing decisions based on last month's prices might pay 10x more than necessary. LiveLLM discovers market changes through SerpApi, verifies them against official sources, and serves compact, provenance-backed economic facts.

what's happening
SerpApi searches Google for current pricing data from official sources. LiveLLM extracts structured facts, validates them against known ranges, and assigns confidence scores. Each fact traces back to its source URL and search query. The result is a verified economic payload that agents can trust.
